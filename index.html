HOW WE ROLL

Five steps. No black box.

You will always know what is happening, what is next, and what we need from you.

01 · THE FIRST CALL

The First Call

Thirty minutes, free, no deck. We ask about your business before we say a word about design. Half the time we tell you that you do not need the thing you came asking for.

02 · DIG

Dig

Competitor teardowns, customer conversations, analytics, ad account audits, and a hard look at what you already have. We come back with what we found, including the uncomfortable parts.

03 · DIRECTION

Direction

One clear strategic route with the thinking shown. Not four options designed to make one look good. If it is wrong, we would rather find out here than in month three.

04 · BUILD

Build

Design, copy, development, shoots, campaigns, systems. Weekly check-ins, a shared board you can open at any time, and one point of contact who actually replies.

05 · SHIP & SHARPEN

Ship & Sharpen

We launch it, watch it, and fix what the data tells us to fix. A launch is a starting line. Anyone who hands over files and disappears was never a partner.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What clients ask before signing.

Written for humans. Also happens to rank well on Google.

What is Active Theory?+

Active Theory is a creative and AI agency with studios in Bengaluru, Karnataka and Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. We work across brand identity, web design and development, performance marketing, and AI development — with deep specialisation in real estate, D2C, and SaaS.

Where are you located?+

Two studios: HSR Layout, Bengaluru — 560 102, and RS Puram, Coimbatore — 641 002. We work remotely with clients elsewhere and travel for launches, shoots, and workshops.

What does a project cost?+

Brand identity projects start at ₹3 lakh. Websites start at ₹2.5 lakh. Performance retainers start at ₹45,000 per month plus ad spend. AI development is scoped per project, typically ₹1.2 lakh upwards. We give you a fixed number after the first call — no hourly surprises.

How long does a project take?+

Brand identity runs four to six weeks. A website runs six to ten weeks depending on page count and integrations. Performance campaigns go live within two weeks of kickoff. AI builds range from three weeks for a qualification agent to three months for a custom internal platform.

Do you work with real estate developers?+

It is one of our three core verticals. We handle project launches end to end — naming, identity, sales collateral, walkthrough films, phase microsites, and the performance campaigns that fill the sales gallery. We report on cost per qualified site visit, not cost per lead.

What does AI development actually mean here?+

Practical systems, not experiments. Lead qualification agents that message an enquiry within minutes, WhatsApp assistants that answer product questions at 11pm, internal tools that kill repetitive work, and AI search optimisation so your brand appears when someone asks ChatGPT for a recommendation.

Do you only work with clients in Bengaluru and Coimbatore?+

No. Around 40% of our work is remote, across India plus clients in UAE and Singapore. Two studios means we can be in the room in person across South India when it matters — which for a project launch, it does.

Who actually works on my project?+

A team of two to four: a strategist, a designer, and depending on scope a developer, copywriter, or media buyer. A founder is on every project. No account manager relaying messages between you and the people doing the work.

What do you need from us to start?+

Access to your existing brand files, analytics, and ad accounts; one decision-maker who can approve work; and roughly two hours a week for reviews. That is genuinely it.

How do we get started?+

Book a 30-minute call at activetheory.in/contact or email hello@activetheory.in. The first conversation is free and there is no deck.
